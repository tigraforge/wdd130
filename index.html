Ondo State

Ondo State is one of the beautiful states in southwestern Nigeria,known for its rich culture, peaceful environment, and natural landscapes. The state was created in 1976 and its capital city is Akure. Ondo State is famous for tourist attractions such as the Idanre Hills, which feature breathtaking rock formations, ancient settlements, and beautiful views of nature. The state is blessed with fertile land that supports farming activities such as cocoa, cassava, yam, and palm oil production. Ondo State also has rivers, forests, and welcoming communities that contribute to its beauty and cultural heritage. The people of Ondo State are known for their hospitality, traditions, and hard work.
