About Me

Hello! My name is Mary Akintade, and I am from Ondo State, Nigeria. I am currently studying software development and building my skills in coding, web development, and UX design. My Coding Journey into tech started with curiosity, but over time it became a dream and a major part of my future goals.
One thing I enjoy most is learning how websites and applications are designed and developed.
I love the creative side of UX design and the logical side of programming because they allow me to combine creativity with problem-solving. Even though learning to code has not always been easy, I continue pushing myself to improve every day.
I believe every project and assignment helps me become a better developer.


Recently, I have been learning Python programming, HTML, CSS, part of my software development fundamentals through my BYU courses. I have worked on assignment involving functions, testing programs, debugging code, and building web pages.

One of the most interesting projects I worked on was creating and fixing a water flow system program using Python.

I also enjoy documenting my coding journey and sharing my progress online on TikTok. Watching myself improve motivates me to continue learning and growing. My goal is to become a software developer who builds meaningful digital products and creates solutions that improve people’s lives.

Ondo State

Ondo State is located in southwestern Nigeria and is known for its rich culture, agriculture, and hardworking people. It is especially famous for cocoa production and natural landscapes.


The people of Ondo State are known for their resilience, creativity, and strong community values. The environment has helped shape my discipline and motivation to pursue excellence in technology and education.
